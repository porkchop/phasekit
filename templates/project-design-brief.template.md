# Design brief

The taste half of this project's UI standard: written by the project's owner, changed only by the
owner's decision. It sets the look and feel; `docs/SPEC.md` still sets the behaviour. The chosen
visual direction is made concrete in `docs/project/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, which build sessions follow.

## Audience

Who the product is for, and on what device first.

## Mood

Three to five words, and what they mean on screen.

## Theme

Light, dark, or following the device (and whether both are designed).

## References

Three to five sites, apps or games whose look to borrow from, and what to take from each.

## What the screens emphasise

The one thing each main screen must make obvious.

## Must not

The product must never feel or behave like this.
